January begins with a disagreement about how much the pilot will matter. Starbucks commits to capacity reservation and measurement. Dutch Bros commits almost its whole assumed monthly envelope to staffing, then spends the remainder on trial credits. Dunkin chooses a breakfast bundle. The customer model expects repeat failures to change its routine, yet assumes the rivals will mostly absorb the extra demand without a major response.

Read those as actual model commitments. The customer’s third action takes a better café experience for granted before any queue result exists. Dunkin’s rationale incorrectly says the 20% reservation is for mobile orders; its committed offer can still be evaluated, but that explanation misreads the proposal. Dutch Bros budgets $28,800 for four added staff-hours per weekday at each of 12 shops, using $30/hour. January 2027 has 21 weekdays: running that staffing plan throughout the month would cost $30,240 before the $1,200 credits. A four-week convention would fit the stated budget, but the game uses calendar months. These are editorial checks against the returned text, not repairs to it.

The accepted initial ruling joins all ten actions in one mechanism. It says time-constrained mobile users redirect to competitors, while café customers benefit. The customer model challenges the first link with d.buyer.b7: coffee at home, postponement and no purchase are also available. Control revises both the causal chain and public history to include those alternatives. Dunkin raises a separate franchise-control objection; control declines that revision because the ruling already described a local opt-in action. Starbucks and Dutch Bros accept.

The revision changes what the next players know: a rejected Starbucks slot no longer implies a sale for a rival. It still does not establish a switching rate. The claimed walk-in benefit remains stronger than the cited preference evidence supports, and neither the challenge round nor schema validation catches the Dutch Bros calendar arithmetic. The final ruling is provisional, carries an unassessed judgment item, and leaves the actual measurement fields empty.
